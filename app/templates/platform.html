How DevTrails is observed

Three connected flows: metrics, Kubernetes logs and email alerting.

01

Metrics

Node Exporter (Ubuntu host) and this app's /metrics → Prometheus (15s scrape) → Grafana dashboards.

02

Kubernetes logs

MicroK8s workloads in the devtrails namespace → Grafana Alloy → Loki → Grafana Explore (LogQL).

03

Alerting

Grafana alert rules (error rate, target down, CPU) → SMTP contact point → the on-call inbox.
